The Basics

What is Mohs surgery?

Mohs micrographic surgery is a precise technique for removing skin cancer, developed by Dr. Frederic Mohs in the 1930s. The cancer is removed one thin layer at a time, and every layer is examined under the microscope right away. If cancer remains at the edge, only that spot is treated again, so surgery stops the moment the margins are clear.

Because 100% of the margin is checked, Mohs surgery has the highest cure rate of any skin cancer treatment, up to 99% for common skin cancers like basal cell and squamous cell carcinoma, while sparing as much healthy skin as possible. That matters most on the face, where every millimeter counts.

Candidacy

Who is a good candidate for Mohs surgery?

Not every skin cancer needs Mohs surgery. Dr. Om will recommend the treatment that's best for your diagnosis and its location. Mohs is often the right choice for:

Face, nose, ears & lips

Cancers on cosmetically and functionally sensitive areas, where sparing healthy tissue protects both appearance and function.

Recurrent skin cancer

Tumors that have come back after a previous treatment.

Aggressive or ill-defined tumors

Cancers with indistinct borders, aggressive growth patterns, or roots that extend beneath the visible surface.

Large lesions & higher-risk patients

Larger tumors, and cancers in patients with weakened immune systems, where complete removal is especially important.

What to Expect

Your Mohs surgery day

Mohs surgery is an outpatient procedure done under local anesthesia, and most cases are completed in a single visit, including reconstruction.

Remove a thin layer

The visible tumor and a thin layer of surrounding tissue are removed under local anesthesia.

Map and stain

The tissue edges are marked with colored dyes and mapped, so any remaining cancer can be located exactly.

Examine every margin

Dr. Om reads the slides himself, checking 100% of the surgical margin under the microscope while you wait.

Repair the site

Once margins are clear, the wound is reconstructed the same day for the best functional and cosmetic outcome.

Mohs Surgery FAQ

Questions about Mohs surgery

What is the cure rate for Mohs surgery?

Mohs surgery has the highest cure rate of any treatment for common skin cancers, up to 99% for basal cell and squamous cell carcinoma. Dr. Om will talk with you about what to expect for your specific diagnosis.

How long does Mohs surgery take?

Plan for a good part of the day. Each layer is processed and read under the microscope while you wait, and most cases finish in a single visit, with reconstruction done the same day.

Will I be awake during Mohs surgery?

Yes. Mohs surgery is performed under local anesthesia as an outpatient procedure. The area is fully numbed, and most patients are comfortable throughout.

Will I have a scar after Mohs surgery?

Any surgery leaves some scar, but Mohs removes the least possible tissue, and Dr. Om specializes in reconstruction designed to hide within natural skin lines. See an honest example on the For Patients page.
